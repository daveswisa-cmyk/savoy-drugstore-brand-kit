# Savoy Drugstore — Prohibited or Review-Required Claims

Do not publish the following unless a specific statement has been separately reviewed and approved in writing by authorised management and, where relevant, compliance counsel.

- Guaranteed product registration, approval, authority acceptance, launch date, processing period, revenue result or market access.
- Any claim of official partnership, endorsement, certification or representation by a UAE authority.
- Medical efficacy, safety, treatment, injectable or therapeutic claims for client products.
- Consumer availability of medical, injectable or professional-use products.
- Client names, logos, testimonials, success metrics, case-study outcomes, product images or timelines without written, context-specific permission.
- Outdated or unverified authority rules, classifications, forms, fees, notices or regulatory pathways.
- Comparative claims about competitors, authorities, products or regulatory outcomes without a substantiated and approved source.
- Unqualified sustainability, clean, natural, compliant, safe, approved, certified or clinically proven claims.

> If a proposed claim is not explicitly in `approved_claims.md`, treat it as **review required** before publishing.
