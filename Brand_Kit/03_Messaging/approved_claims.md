## Authority Wording Rule

Use **MOHAP** and **Dubai Municipality (DM)** only where relevant to the product category and after a current regulatory check. Otherwise write **relevant UAE authorities**. Do not imply an official partnership, a guaranteed authority path, registration, approval or market-access outcome.
