# Savoy Drugstore Visual System

Savoy uses a professional, precise visual language that combines regulatory authority with an elevated aesthetics perspective. Layouts should be spacious, editorial and readable rather than consumer-retail focused.

## Core Principles

Use navy for authority, blue for controlled emphasis, white or off-white for clarity, and gold only as a limited premium accent. Use Playfair Display for principal editorial headings and Inter for all operational copy. Section tags may be uppercase with restrained tracking; process diagrams may use leading-zero numbering.

## Social Direction

Instagram should use photorealistic professional imagery such as a modern UAE office, controlled documentation review, laboratory-adjacent surfaces, brand-planning environments or professional clinic-context cues. Overlay a concise branded text panel or high-contrast text area and a transparent logo. Alternate layout types so two consecutive posts do not look the same. Do not use generic consumer beauty imagery, dark luxury-only imagery, crowded compositions, unreadable overlays or uncontrolled AI hands/text.

LinkedIn should use clean white, navy and blue hierarchy, clear headline-led visuals and disciplined B2B language. External website links belong in the first comment rather than the post body.

## Accessibility Baseline

Use high contrast for type over photography, preserve legible type scale, avoid communicating an outcome with colour alone, add descriptive alt text, and use readable text alternatives for any motion or carousel content.
