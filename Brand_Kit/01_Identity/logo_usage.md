# Savoy Drugstore Logo Usage

## Approved Variants

| File | Use | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `logo_primary_transparent.png` | Standard identity placement on light and neutral backgrounds | Use at a readable horizontal size. |
| `logo_light_transparent.png` | Dark photography, navy panels and dark web surfaces | Use only where contrast is sufficient. |
| `logo_dark_transparent.png` | Reference alias for standard dark/navy treatment on light backgrounds | Use at a readable horizontal size. |
| `logo_gold_transparent.png` | Owner-approved premium accent treatments | Use sparingly; never as the default social logo. |

All files are approved transparent PNG binaries. They must be used without redrawing, recolouring, cropping the wordmark, adding effects, or placing opaque rectangles behind them.

## Placement

Maintain clear space equal to at least the height of the `S` in the wordmark on every side. For social posts, use the horizontal wordmark at a small but readable size; place it away from interface overlays and preserve visual contrast. Dark imagery requires the white variant. Light or white layouts require the primary navy variant.

## Prohibited Uses

Do not use screenshots, JPEG conversions, stretched marks, unapproved recolours, AI-generated logo approximations, logo-only watermarks that hide legal copy, or external third-party marks without documented permission.
